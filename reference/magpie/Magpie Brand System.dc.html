02The mark

The mark

Refined from the starting-point geometry: a distinct head lobe with a throat notch instead of one merged blob, the eye moved into the head just behind the beak, the shoulder patch shrunk to 36% of body width and moved onto the wing so a light–dark rhythm survives downscaling, and the tail now leaves the body's rear edge and tapers to a near-point over a longer, shallower sweep.

Primary
#712D85 on light. Patch and eye are the background, not white ink.
Reversed
White on #712D85. 8.68:1.
Mono
One colour for print, foil, engraving. Knockouts stay open.

Minimum size 16 px, and 16 px is a separate drawing. The 16 px optical variant drops the eye, thickens head and tail by roughly 8%, and widens the shoulder patch – the patch is the only knockout that survives, and it is what makes the shape read as a bird rather than a dot. From 24 px up, the full mark is used. Below 16 px, wordmark alone.

Clear space

Clear space on all four sides equals the height of the shoulder patch – 0.16 × mark height. Nothing enters it, including the wordmark of a parent VMO2 lockup.

05bAnimated mark

Animated mark

Four states, one bird. Motion is small, slow, and always loops back to rest – the interviewee is talking about their job, not watching a mascot. Every state has a static fallback under prefers-reduced-motion.

Listening

3.2 s bob, 6% travel, plus a blink every 5 s. Runs while the interviewee is speaking.

Gathering

Tail sweeps −7° over 2.2 s. Body stays still. Pairs with the rotating craft word.

Processing

Quiet-blend silhouette drifting 10% horizontally, 2.6 s. Silhouette only – this is the one state without knockouts.

Kept

A single hop on save, then rest. Plays once in product – looping here for reference.

Motion rules. No rotation of the whole mark, no bounce past rest, no flight. Amplitude stays under 14% of mark height. Nothing animates below 24 px – the movement reads as a rendering fault. One animated mark on screen at a time, and never while the interviewee is mid-answer except Listening.

07Guidelines

Brand guidelines

Story

Magpies gather and keep valuable things. Magpie gathers scattered knowledge – the parts of a job that live in people's heads and leave the building when they do – and keeps it safe as verified process specifications, maps, and recommendations. The colour is the channel-wise midpoint of O2 Blue and Virgin Media Red: the product sits at the intersection of the two brands, and its colour is literally made from both.

Logo rules

  • Clear space = shoulder-patch height on all sides.
  • Minimum mark size 16 px; below that, wordmark only.
  • Never outline, rotate, stretch, or recolour outside purple / white / single-colour mono. The one exception is the heritage-blend plate behind a white mark, at 64 px and above.
  • Negative space is the background showing through – never a painted white shape on a photo.
  • Wordmark is always sentence case: Magpie, never MAGPIE or magpie.
  • The VMO2 capsule-and-endcap motif carries Magpie's section labels: purple capsule, O2 Blue endcap circle overlapping the right edge by 40% of its diameter. Purple capsule with a Virgin Media Red endcap is not used – the pairing reads as a status.
  • Magpie never appears beside the VMO2 corporate infinity lockup in a single lockup. Where both are needed, they sit at opposite ends of the page.

The never-rules

  • Magpie purple never carries semantic meaning – no success, warning, or error.
  • Magpie purple never appears on an interactive control. O2 Blue stays primary.
  • Virgin Media Red never appears in a loading or processing state.
  • Only two gradients exist – the heritage blend and the quiet blend. No other gradient, no decorative shadow, no texture.
  • The blend never sits behind reading text, never on a control, never more than once per screen.
  • Do not restyle VMO2 core components. Magpie is a layer, not a replacement.

Licensed Aeonik Pro web fonts are wired from the VMO2 estate bundle – Light 300, Regular 400, Medium 500, Bold 700, Black 900. Display moments may use Black uppercase per the VMO2 toolkit, but Magpie's own wordmark stays Medium, sentence case.

Tone – do

  • Describe Magpie's craft: gathering, weaving, cross-checking, distilling.
  • Sentence case everywhere. British English.
  • Plain, calm, specific: "Four interviews, twenty-one recommendations."
  • Credit the person: "Thanks – that's the part no document has."
  • Say what happens next, in one line.

Tone – don't

  • Judge the process: "inefficient", "workaround", "should be".
  • Describe the user's work as messy, undocumented, or a gap.
  • Imply the output feeds a headcount decision.
  • Title Case, exclamation marks, emoji, or American spelling.
  • Marketing adjectives: "seamless", "powerful", "intelligent".

Accessibility commitments

  • Every text/background pairing in this system meets WCAG 2.2 AA; ratios are stated beside each pairing.
  • Colour is never the only carrier of meaning – status uses a label as well as a tone.
  • The loading cross-fade and all four animated mark states respect prefers-reduced-motion: a static "Working…" in Magpie purple, no colour cycling, no movement.
  • The heritage blend is decorative only – no text, control, or status relies on a position within it.
  • Focus rings follow the VMO2 base system, 2 px O2 Blue with 2 px offset, on both light and dark surfaces.
  • Interview transcripts are keyboard-navigable and screen-reader ordered; the Stop recording control is always the first focusable element on the interview screen.
