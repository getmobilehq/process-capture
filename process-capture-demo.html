01Process identity & contextAnswered

Resolution of customer complaints reaching the contact centre – primarily billing disputes and service issues – including goodwill payments where the business is at fault. Starts when a case lands in the CRM complaints queue; ends when the case is closed with a wrap-up code and the customer has accepted the resolution.

05Workflow & activitiesAnswered

  1. Case arrives in the CRM complaints queue – advisor picks up in queue order
  2. Review account history in the billing system – actor: advisor · system: billing platform
  3. Call the customer back within 24 hours – agree the facts and the proposed resolution
  4. Decision: goodwill credit required? → raise credit request (see facet 6) / proceed to close
  5. Handoff: billing adjustments confirmed by the billing team – known queue point
  6. Close the case with wrap-up code; customer notified

06Business rules & decisionsAnswered

  • Goodwill up to £25 – advisor approves directly
  • £25–£100 – team leader approval
  • Over £100 – operations manager approval
  • Over £500 – weekly review panel
Finding · routed to process owner
